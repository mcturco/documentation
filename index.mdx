Kong Air provides a suite of REST APIs that let you search scheduled flights, look up routes, create and manage bookings, and access customer profile data — all through a single base URL. Whether you're building a travel app, an airline integration, or a third-party service, this portal has everything you need to authenticate, explore, and call the Kong Air APIs.

## Get Up and Running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request API access">
    Sign up for a Kong Air developer account to obtain your API key or JWT credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate your requests">
    Pass your API key in the `X-API-Key` header, or use a Bearer JWT in the `Authorization` header on every request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the APIs">
    Use the [Quickstart](/quickstart) guide to make your first call, then explore the [API Reference](/api/flights/get-flights) for the full surface area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your integration">
    Follow the [Guides](/guides/search-flights) for end-to-end walkthroughs of the most common use cases.
  </Step>
</Steps>
